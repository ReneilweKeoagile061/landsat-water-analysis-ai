I am requesting access to the geospatial borehole point dataset covering the Stampriet Transboundary Aquifer System (STAS), shared between Botswana, Namibia, and South Africa (referenced in UNESCO-IHP / AmeriGEOSS layer 'geonode:boreholestas').

1. Information Requested:
   - Complete georeferenced borehole layer (GeoJSON, ESRI Shapefile, or CSV) with spatial coordinates, country codes, total depth (m), static water level (m), and recorded borehole yields (m³/h or l/s).
   - Attribute metadata detailing whether recorded yields reflect step-drawdown pump tests or airlift estimations.
   - Clarification on licensing terms for hydrogeological research and decision-support modeling.

2. Context of Research:
   - This data will serve as a transboundary regional benchmark to evaluate remote-sensing groundwater prospectivity indicators across the Kalahari basin.
   - Findings will be shared with relevant transboundary water resources bodies to support sustainable regional groundwater governance.
