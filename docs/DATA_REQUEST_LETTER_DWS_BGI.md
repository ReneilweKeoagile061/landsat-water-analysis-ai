RE: REQUEST FOR GROUNDWATER BOREHOLE COMPLETION CERTIFICATES (IGWRMS), 1:1M STRUCTURAL GEOLOGY VECTORS, AND REGIONAL AEROMAGNETIC DATA

I am writing to formally request access to national geoscience and hydrogeological data to support computational research and development of remote-sensing groundwater siting and recharge potential models across Botswana's arid and semi-arid terrains (including the Kalahari, Okavango, and transitional basement zones).

1. Data Requested:
   a. Borehole Completion Records (IGWRMS / DWS / BGI BoreHive):
      - National borehole records containing georeferenced coordinates (WGS84), total drilled depth (m), static water level (m), and water strike depth (m).
      - Certified yield logs (m³/h or l/s), pump test duration/drawdown measurements, and driller's test records.
      - Critically: Records of unsuccessful / abandoned / dry drillings to serve as objective ground-truth negative labels.
   b. Structural Geology Shapefiles (1:1M National Geological Map):
      - Vector shapefiles for Dolerite Dykes (Layer: BWA_DGS_1M_MLF).
      - Vector shapefiles for Faults and Structural Shear Zones (Layer: BWA_DGS_1M_MSF).
   c. Regional Airborne Geophysical Grids:
      - Total Magnetic Intensity (TMI) and first vertical derivative (1VD) grids for available blocks (e.g. Nossop-Ncojane, Molopo Farms, and Moiyabana survey areas) for structural lineament mapping beneath superficial Kalahari sand cover.

2. Purpose & Licensing:
   - The data will be used to calibrate spatial machine-learning and hydrogeological screening tools designed to assist local farmers and hydrogeologists in prioritizing field geophysics (VES / ERT) and mitigating dry borehole drilling costs.
   - We request formal guidance on commercial-use licensing, data sharing agreements, or research-partnership terms with the Government of Botswana.

We would be grateful for an opportunity to discuss terms, required administrative fees, or a formal Memorandum of Understanding.
