      Matched Actions & Siting Strategy

      • Borehole Siting (Sandy Loam / High TWI): Target deep permeable recharge fractures with low clay capping for reliable yield.
      • Earth Dams & Ponds (High Clay >30%): Dig lined reservoirs in natural depressions where soil prevents seepage.
      • Contour Swales (Slopes 2–5°): Slow runoff to force infiltration and recharge root-zone water tables.
      • Soil Sponge Mulching (Sandy Uplands): Organic mulch to arrest rapid drainage and minimize evaporation.
